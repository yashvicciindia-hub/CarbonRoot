Business Model

Multiple Revenue Streams

Our farmer-first revenue sharing model directs up to 70% of carbon credit value directly to the farmers implementing sustainable practices.

Farmer-first revenue share

70%

This approach fosters trust, drives large-scale adoption, and ensures high retention among our farmer partners. Our technology infrastructure, with its fixed-cost nature, ensures that unit economics improve dramatically as transaction volumes increase.

Revenue Streams

Four ways CarbonRoot generates revenue

01

Take Rate

We earn a 15–25% commission on every carbon credit sold, providing a scalable revenue stream that grows with market volume.

02

MRV-as-a-Service

SaaS subscription model for NGOs, CSR projects, and agricultural cooperatives needing verification services.

03

Marketplace Commissions

Additional buyer-side transaction fees for premium services, reporting, and impact verification.

04

Premium Credits

Higher margins on curated co-benefit-linked projects focused on gender equity, biodiversity, and water conservation.
