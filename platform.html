Aggregator Console

A powerful dashboard empowering Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and cooperatives to manage bulk farmer onboarding, pre-validate sustainable practices, and visualize comprehensive MRV data across all member farms.

Bulk onboarding Pre-validation MRV visualization

MRV Engine

Our advanced analytics platform leverages satellite NDVI data, AI-powered yield & soil carbon inference, and blockchain-based smart contracts for tamper-proof verification of carbon sequestration.

Satellite NDVI AI yield/soil inference Smart contracts
