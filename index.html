The Gap

India's carbon potential is real — and largely untapped

Despite being the 3rd largest carbon emitter globally, India's smallholder farmers remain locked out of the voluntary carbon market by fragmentation, costly verification, and a lasting trust deficit.

01

Fragmentation

Millions of smallholder farms struggle to monetize carbon credits due to lack of scale and inadequate infrastructure.

02

Expensive MRV

Monitoring, Reporting, and Verification is prohibitively costly, slow, and opaque for individual farmers.

03

Trust Deficit

Global buyers lack confidence in the quality and verifiable nature of Indian carbon credits.

04

Unfair Distribution

Over 80% of carbon revenue fails to reach the farmers who implement sustainable practices.

Our Solution

CarbonRoot: India's bottom-up carbon origination platform

A full-stack platform that onboards smallholder farmers, verifies impact with satellite and IoT data, and connects directly to the global voluntary carbon market — with up to 70% of revenue reaching farmers.

01

Aggregation

Small & marginal farmers organized through FPOs, SHGs, and cooperatives.

02

Verification

Satellite imagery, IoT sensors, and mobile-first data collection in local languages.

03

Traceability

Blockchain-based tracking from farm to buyer, for full transparency.

04

Connection

Direct integration with VERRA, Gold Standard, and international carbon markets.
