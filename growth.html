Go-To-Market & Financials

Growth: Phased Expansion, Path to Profitability

Our regional focus allows us to build deep expertise in specific agroecological zones before expanding, ensuring quality and credibility of the carbon credits we produce.

Go-To-Market Strategy

Phased Expansion

Year 1 — Foundation

10 FPOs, 100,000+ acres

We will establish partnerships with 10 Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) across Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, and Odisha, covering over 100,000 acres of agricultural land.

Simultaneously, we will develop and deploy our core technology stack, including the farmer app, aggregator console, and initial MRV systems.

Year 2 — Validation

200,000 verified credits, first international buyers

Our focus shifts to issuing 200,000 verified carbon credits and onboarding our first international buyers through pilot programs in Singapore and key European markets.

We will also refine our MRV methodologies based on real-world data and feedback, optimizing for accuracy and cost-efficiency.

Year 3 — Expansion

Forest fringe areas & biochar projects

Operations will extend to forest fringe areas, and we will launch specialized biochar projects, leveraging their higher carbon sequestration potential.

Our partner network will be scaled to include strategic collaborations with NABARD, Krishi Vigyan Kendras, regional NGOs, and corporate CSR initiatives.

Financial Projections

Path to Profitability

We are strategically positioned to reach break-even by mid-Year 3. Our advanced technology platform is designed for significant economies of scale, ensuring that marginal costs approach zero once the core MRV infrastructure is fully established and operational within a region.
