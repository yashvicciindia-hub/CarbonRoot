Who We Are

Mission, solution, and vision — in one place

CarbonRoot exists to close the gap between India's smallholder farmers and the global carbon economy.

MISSION

Empowering farmers through a bottom-up carbon platform

To empower India's smallholder farmers by building the nation's first bottom-up carbon origination platform, directly linking them to the global voluntary carbon market and ensuring fair carbon revenue distribution.

SOLUTION

A full-stack platform built for scale and trust

Our full-stack platform efficiently onboards smallholder farmers, leverages cutting-edge technology to generate low-cost, verifiable carbon credits, and guarantees up to 70% of the revenue directly to the farmers.

Full-stack platform Smallholder farmer onboarding Verifiable carbon credits Up to 70% revenue to farmers
VISION

Indian villages as contributors to the global carbon economy

A future where Indian villages become key contributors to the global carbon economy, minting carbon credits and unlocking the nation's vast potential while empowering its climate-vulnerable populations.

From Village to Global Market

Indian villages → carbon economy → climate action → rural empowerment

Every stage of CarbonRoot's platform is built around one journey: helping a smallholder farm participate credibly in a market it has historically been excluded from — and keeping the value of that participation with the farmer.

Building India's next-gen carbon credit infrastructure — linking smallholder farmers to the global voluntary carbon market.
