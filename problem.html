The Problem

India's Untapped Carbon Potential

Despite being the 3rd largest carbon emitter globally and possessing immense carbon offset potential, India's participation in the voluntary carbon market faces significant barriers.

01

Fragmentation

Millions of smallholder farms struggle to monetize carbon credits due to lack of scale and inadequate infrastructure.

02

Expensive MRV

Monitoring, Reporting, and Verification processes are prohibitively costly, slow, and opaque for individual farmers.

03

Trust Deficit

Global buyers lack confidence in the quality and verifiable nature of Indian carbon credits.

04

Unfair Distribution

Over 80% of carbon revenue fails to reach the farmers who implement sustainable practices.

The Consequence

India's vast carbon mitigation potential remains severely undersupplied

The result: India's vast carbon mitigation potential remains severely undersupplied to the global voluntary carbon market, leaving billions in potential revenue unrealized.
